AION 2 dungeon boss guide

Fire Temple boss mechanics

Kromede’s Desire fills the room with fire. Two mechanics, both about where you stand — and how long you have to keep moving.

Exploration is the easy version, Conquest the hard one. Calls are what the A2Naki boss mod says out loud; our recordings are from Exploration, so on Conquest the same mechanics are unconfirmed until we have recorded it.

Kromede’s Desire

The boss

  • Blazing Judgment* — the fire walls

    Kromede takes a buff, and walls of fire keep rising across the room, one after another.

    Do: keep moving and stand where no wall is rising; heal through it rather than chasing the boss. Call: Fire walls! Find the gap with a countdown that ends with the last wall

  • Ground Fire*

    Patches of fire appear all over the floor and burn briefly. It comes both before and after the fire walls.

    Do: step off the fire at once. Call: Dodge!

Ran Fire Temple on Conquest? Record a pull and share it in the beta on Discord.

About the spell names. The game doesn’t show English names for most boss spells, so we name them ourselves — after what the spell does, or translated from the Korean client. Every name with a * is one of ours, not the game’s. Spells we haven’t named yet are described by what they do.
