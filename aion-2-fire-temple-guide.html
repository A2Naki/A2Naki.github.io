Fire Temple boss mechanics

Kromede’s Desire is a positioning fight. Both of the boss’s mechanics put fire on the ground, and the group needs to keep moving to stay out of it.

Exploration is the easy difficulty and Conquest the hard one. The Calls under each mechanic are the warnings the A2Naki boss mod says out loud. Our recordings are from Exploration, so on Conquest the same mechanics are unconfirmed until we have recorded it.

Kromede’s Desire

The boss

  • Blazing Judgment*, the fire walls

    The boss gains a buff and walls of fire begin to rise across the room, one after another. Players need to keep moving and stand where no wall is rising, and healers should focus on keeping the group alive through this phase rather than chasing the boss.

    Call: Fire walls! Find the gap, with a countdown that ends with the last wall

  • Ground Fire*

    Patches of fire appear across the floor and burn for a short time. This happens both before and after the fire walls. Step off the fire immediately.

    Call: Dodge!

If you have run Fire Temple on Conquest, please record a pull and share it in the beta on Discord.

About the ability names. The game does not show English names for most boss abilities, so we name them ourselves, either after what the ability does or as a translation from the Korean client. Names marked with a * are ours and not official. Abilities we have not named yet are described instead.
