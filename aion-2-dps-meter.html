What the DPS meter shows

Every number comes from the game itself. If we don’t know something, the app says so instead of guessing.

I · In the fight

In the fight

  • DPS & healing meterDamage, rDPS, healing, taken, skills and utility for the whole group, your own row in gold. Rates with two decimals, so 9.33k and 9.41k are not the same number.
  • Boss HP and cast barsHP with a trailing damage chunk, the stagger gauge, every cast as it happens with the interruptible ones marked, and a kill projection that tells you whether you beat the enrage.
  • Spoken boss-mechanic warnings, boss by bossCalls like “Stop DPS soon!” or “Kael marked, move to Kael!”, written per boss from our recorded pulls and shown on screen as well as said out loud. You choose which ones you want.
  • Fight summary & historyKill or wipe, time vs your best, top damage and healing, deaths with the last hits that caused them, and who resurrected whom.
  • Dungeon & EventsSwitch from one pull to the whole dungeon run, world boss or field event, through respawns and group changes. Cleared runs keep your records (best DPS, best HPS and fastest clear), and your row shows how you’re doing against them.
  • Meter overlay in the gameJust the meter, in a small see-through window on top of the game. Clicks go straight through to the game; Ctrl+Shift+1–8 switch tabs. Needs Borderless or Windowed mode.
  • Copy to chatOne tap copies the list you’re looking at as plain text, ready to paste into party chat or Discord.
II · Your group

Your group

  • PartyHP and mana for everyone, low-HP and death alerts that stay until you tap them, people asking to join.
  • Buff timersBuffs, debuffs and cooldowns, shortest first; the last five seconds blink.
  • Combat StatsYour character’s stats and deity points, as far as the game sends them.
III · Between fights

Between fights

  • Kinah per hourEarned, spent and net for the session, with a sparkline.
  • CraftingLevels, recipes and materials as you work.
  • FarmingYour pets: the souls you got this session and how far each pet is to its next level. Then the daily and weekly Homework per character, Od energy and tickets, reset by itself.
  • World timersShugo Festival, invasions, rifts and sieges in server time and your local time, for your server region.
  • Reminders, out loudReminders such as “Od energy is full” or the daily reset, read aloud if you want and never in the middle of a fight. Off until you switch them on.
IV · Getting better

Training

  • Training on the dummyHit a Punching Bag or a Training Scarecrow and the target card becomes a training card: pick a timer of 30 s to 5 minutes, the clock starts on your first hit and stops when the timer runs out, and you see your DPS against your last run and your best at the same second.
  • Every run kept with your setupRotation, combat power, item level, stats, buffs and gear are saved with each run. A run ends when the timer runs out; press Restart training for the next one. The last 100 runs per character stay.
  • Which piece changedPut two runs side by side: the rows that differ are marked, down to the gear slot you swapped or upgraded.
  • Analysis pageIn the browser: DPS over the fight with its axes, your rotation per skill with the gaps where a skill was ready and unused, two fights skill by skill, and what stopped you and for how long.

Built-in meter or A2Naki?

AION 2’s own damage meter vs the A2Naki DPS meter

AION 2 has a personal damage meter of its own. Use it if your own number is all you want. A2Naki is for when you want to see the whole group.

What you seeIn-game meterA2Naki DPS meter
Whose damageYoursEveryone in your party, your own row in gold
NumbersDamage and DPS, per skill in detailed modeDamage, rDPS, healing, damage taken, skills (hits, crits, back attacks), utility (interrupts, cleanses)
After the fightNoKill or wipe, time vs your best, deaths with the last hits, dungeon records
BossNoBoss HP, cast bars, stagger gauge, kill projection against the enrage, spoken boss-mechanic warnings
PracticeNoTraining runs on the dummy, two runs compared down to the gear slot
WhereIn the game windowSee-through overlay, any browser, or a Corsair Xeneon Edge

rDPS gives the extra damage from a group damage buff to the player who cast the buff, so a support player’s share is not hidden in the top damage dealer’s number.

How it works

A small app in your Windows tray reads the data the game server already sends to your PC and shows it on screen.

  1. Game serverSends your client the fight, party and world data, as always.
  2. Your PCNpcap lets the app receive a copy. Nothing is sent, injected or read from memory.
  3. 127.0.0.1The app talks only to your own dashboard, locked with a token only your Windows user can read.
  4. Browser, Edge or overlayOn any browser, as Widgets in iCUE, or as a meter on top of the game

Passive

Receive-only. It never sends anything to the game and never touches game files or memory.

Read-only

It shows what the game tells your PC. There is no automation and no macros, and it never plays for you.

Stays on your PC

No account, no telemetry. Nothing leaves your PC unless you choose to send it, for example a bug report.

Still: NCSOFT may see reading game traffic as against the AION 2 terms of service, which is a risk to your account. Read Is it safe? before you decide.

No Edge? Any screen works

The browser page puts pages 1 and 2 together and fits any window, so you can use a second monitor, a laptop or a tablet.

You decide which windows you have open. Encounter, Party, Farming, World, Kinah and the others are separate widgets, so you can run anything from a single meter on top of the game to the whole dashboard.
