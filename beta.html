A2Naki Companion is a free fan-made dashboard for AION 2, in any browser or on a Corsair Xeneon Edge: a damage / healing meter, boss HP, cast bars and spoken boss-mechanic warnings, party HP, buff timers, your Combat Stats, Kinah per hour, crafting progress, daily / weekly homework, world timers and much more.

Who we are looking for

Profile – what you would test

We want English-speaking testers who play AION 2 differently from each other, so the app gets tested everywhere, not just in one dungeon. Tell us on Discord which of these fit you (more than one is fine):

  • End-game / dungeon runner

    Conquest, the newest dungeons – Boss warnings, wipe checks, the meter, fight summary and history, recordings of bosses we have not seen yet

  • Healer

    Party HP and death alerts, cleanse warnings, healing meter, resurrections in the summary

  • Tank

    Targeted and group-damage warnings, damage taken, interrupts

  • Levelling / new characters

    Open-world fights, quest instances, the Character card, XP and level-ups

  • Farmer / Kinah maker

    Kinah per hour, long sessions, open-world fights and field events, world bosses

  • Crafter / gatherer

    Crafting levels, recipes and materials, gathering

  • Alt-heavy player

    Several characters on one PC: homework per character, Od, tickets, switching characters

  • Group / legion organiser

    Party list (people joining late, leaving), legion page, field bosses with a full group

Also useful, in any combination: With and without a Xeneon Edge (the browser page runs on any screen, tablet or phone). Windows 10 and Windows 11.

What we ask

  • Play as you normally do with the app running, and tell us on Discord what works and what doesn’t.
  • Switch on recording when we ask for a specific thing (“Krao Cave on Conquest”, “a world boss”), and send bug reports with the template.
  • Keep the beta builds on our Discord: don’t re-upload them elsewhere. Point people to the Discord instead.

Please know before you join

  • We want to build an app that helps AION 2 players – we hope you want to help us.
  • The app only listens to traffic, but NCSOFT may still see that as against the AION 2 terms of service. That is a risk to your account. You decide.
  • It needs Npcap (free) and Windows may show a SmartScreen warning (the app is not code-signed yet).
  • Recordings contain your character and the names of players near you; we only use them to improve the app and delete them after analysis. You choose when to record.
