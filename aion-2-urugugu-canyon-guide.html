Urugugu Canyon boss mechanics

A boss that punishes every hit, a judge you must back away from, and Divine Auldor’s marks. Recorded on Conquest.

Exploration is the easy version, Conquest the hard one. Calls are what the A2Naki boss mod says out loud; everything here is from Conquest pulls, and Kael stands for the player the boss picked.

Guardian Captain Raur

First boss

  • ConquestCounter stance

    Raur takes a stance. Every hit on him is answered at the player who hit — and each answer hits harder than the last.

    Do: stop hitting him and step away until the stance ends. Call: Stop DPS, run away from boss!

  • ConquestThe cast at a player

    Raur casts at one player, again and again through the fight.

    Do: the player runs out from the boss. Call: Move away from boss!

Judge Urahum

Second boss

  • ConquestThe zone around the judge

    One cast starts it: Urahum pulses around himself and a zone follows.

    Do: get away from the boss as soon as the cast starts. Call: Get out away from boss! — one call, nothing else on this boss

Divine Auldor

Last boss

  • ConquestSummon Whirlwind* — the mark

    One player is marked. When the mark ends, Storm Strike* explodes on that player, and the damage is shared by everyone close by.

    Do: everyone stacks on the marked player before it explodes. Call: Kael marked, move to Kael!

  • ConquestWing Strike*

    Auldor casts it on himself — the spell he uses most.

    Do: dodge it. Call: Dodge!

  • ConquestThe debuff near him

    A cast on himself puts a debuff on everyone standing close.

    Do: step away from the boss. Call: Move away from boss!

  • ConquestSnatch*

    Auldor casts at one player — a different one each time — and grabs them: two hits and a debuff.

    Do: the named player moves out at once; healers get ready. Call: Kael targeted, move out!

Ran Urugugu Canyon on Exploration? Record a pull and share it in the beta on Discord.

About the spell names. The game doesn’t show English names for most boss spells, so we name them ourselves — after what the spell does, or translated from the Korean client. Every name with a * is one of ours, not the game’s. Spells we haven’t named yet are described by what they do.
