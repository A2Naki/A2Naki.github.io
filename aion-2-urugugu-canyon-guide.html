Urugugu Canyon boss mechanics

Urugugu Canyon has three bosses. Guardian Captain Raur punishes every hit during his counter stance, Judge Urahum has to be kept at a distance, and Divine Auldor marks players. Everything on this page comes from Conquest pulls.

Exploration is the easy difficulty and Conquest the hard one. The Calls under each mechanic are the warnings the A2Naki boss mod says out loud. Kael stands for the player the boss picks.

Guardian Captain Raur

First boss

  • ConquestCounter stance

    Raur enters a counter stance. Every hit he takes is answered with a hit on the attacker, and each answer is stronger than the previous one. Stop attacking and move away until the stance ends.

    Call: Stop DPS, run away from boss!

  • ConquestTargeted cast

    Raur repeatedly casts at a single player throughout the fight. The targeted player should move away from the boss.

    Call: Move away from boss!

Judge Urahum

Second boss

  • ConquestZone around the boss

    A single cast starts this mechanic: Urahum pulses damage around himself and a zone follows. Players should move away from the boss as soon as the cast starts. It is the only mechanic on this boss that the boss mod calls out.

    Call: Get out away from boss!

Divine Auldor

Last boss

  • ConquestSummon Whirlwind*, the mark

    Auldor marks a single player. When the mark expires, Storm Strike* explodes on that player and the damage is split between everyone standing nearby, so the whole group should stack on the marked player before the explosion.

    Call: Kael marked, move to Kael!

  • ConquestWing Strike*

    Wing Strike* is the ability Auldor uses most often. Players should move out of it.

    Call: Dodge!

  • ConquestDebuff around the boss

    Auldor casts on himself and applies a debuff to every player standing close to him. Players near him should step away when the cast starts.

    Call: Move away from boss!

  • ConquestSnatch*

    Auldor targets a player, a different one each time, and grabs them, which deals two hits and applies a debuff. The named player should move out at once, and healers should be ready to heal them.

    Call: Kael, run out!

If you have run Urugugu Canyon on Exploration, please record a pull and share it in the beta on Discord.

About the ability names. The game does not show English names for most boss abilities, so we name them ourselves, either after what the ability does or as a translation from the Korean client. Names marked with a * are ours and not official. Abilities we have not named yet are described instead.
