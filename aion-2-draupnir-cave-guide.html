AION 2 dungeon boss guide

Draupnir Cave boss mechanics

Reflect, orbs you can’t kill, and a last boss with two wipe checks on Conquest. Here is the whole fight, boss by boss.

Exploration is the easy version, Conquest the hard one. Calls are what the A2Naki boss mod says out loud.

Phantasmal Lakshmi

First boss

  • ExplorationConquestReflect*

    Lakshmi takes a buff that sends the group’s damage back at it. It comes at around three-quarters HP and again later in the fight.

    Do: stop attacking until the buff is gone. Call: Stop attacking with a countdown

  • ExplorationConquestSummon Handmaiden*

    Under half HP she summons Lakshmi’s Handmaiden.

    Do: switch to the Handmaiden and kill her. Call: Adds!

  • ConquestThe fast hit

    A cast that lands almost as soon as it starts, so there is very little warning.

    Do: run out the moment you hear it. Call: Run out!

Predator Saraswati

Second boss

  • ExplorationConquestOrb Summon*

    A few orbs land on the floor and hit everyone near them with Orb Pulse*. You can’t kill them — on Conquest they are the main reason groups wipe.

    Do: move the fight away from the orbs and keep the boss there until they fade. Call: Move Away!

  • ExplorationConquestSingle Orb*

    One orb that pulses with Orb Pulse* just like the others.

    Do: keep out of its reach until it fades. Call: Move Away!

Transcendent Bakarma

Last boss

  • ExplorationConquestWave of Death: Call* — the first wipe check

    Bakarma calls and a stagger gauge appears; Death Pulse* hits the group while it is up. If the gauge holds, Wave of Death* kills the whole group. On Conquest it comes at around three-quarters HP, and the groups that made it broke the gauge at the very last moment.

    Do: everyone saves stagger and control skills for this and uses them at once. Call: Break the gauge with stagger skills! with a countdown

  • ExplorationConquestSwarm Roar*

    A long cast. Swarm Mark* marks every player over and over, and Swarm Sting* water balls fall where the players just stood. On Conquest it comes once, after the first wipe check.

    Do: keep moving for the whole cast — never stop where you just were. Call: Avoid!

  • ConquestThe leap and two waves

    Bakarma casts on himself, and later two waves roll out, one right after the other.

    Do: jump over both waves. Call: Jump Waves, twice!

  • ConquestWinding Waterspout*

    At around a third of his HP, waves form around the boss and keep coming.

    Do: jump over the waves as they reach you. Call: Avoid Waves, Jump!

  • ConquestWave of Death: Call* — the second wipe check

    Right after the Waterspout Bakarma calls again, with the same gauge to break. It never comes on Exploration, and it is where most Conquest groups wipe.

    Do: have your stagger skills back off cooldown by the time he is at a third of his HP. Call: Break the gauge with stagger skills!

Spotted something different in your pull? Tell us on Discord.

About the spell names. The game doesn’t show English names for most boss spells, so we name them ourselves — after what the spell does, or translated from the Korean client. Every name with a * is one of ours, not the game’s. Spells we haven’t named yet are described by what they do.
