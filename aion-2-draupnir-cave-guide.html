Draupnir Cave boss mechanics

Draupnir Cave has three bosses. Phantasmal Lakshmi reflects damage, Predator Saraswati covers the floor with orbs that cannot be killed, and Transcendent Bakarma has two wipe checks on Conquest.

Exploration is the easy difficulty and Conquest the hard one. The Calls under each mechanic are the warnings the A2Naki boss mod says out loud.

Phantasmal Lakshmi

First boss

  • ExplorationConquestReflect*

    Lakshmi gains a buff that reflects the group’s damage back at the attackers. It first appears at around three-quarters health and returns later in the fight. All players should stop attacking until the buff has expired.

    Call: Stop attacking, with a countdown on screen

  • ExplorationConquestSummon Handmaiden*

    Below half health Lakshmi summons Lakshmi’s Handmaiden. The group should switch to the Handmaiden and kill her before returning to the boss.

    Call: Adds!

  • ConquestFast hit

    This cast lands almost immediately after it starts, which leaves very little time to react. Run out as soon as the call comes.

    Call: Run out!

Predator Saraswati

Second boss

  • ExplorationConquestOrb Summon*

    Saraswati places several orbs on the floor, and Orb Pulse* damages every player near them. The orbs cannot be killed and in our Conquest pull they killed several players, so the tank should move the boss away from them and keep the fight there until they fade.

    Call: Move!

  • ExplorationConquestSingle Orb*

    A single orb that pulses with Orb Pulse* in the same way. Players should stay out of its range until it fades.

    Call: Move!

Transcendent Bakarma

Last boss

  • ExplorationConquestWave of Death: Call*, the first wipe check

    Bakarma casts Wave of Death: Call* and a stagger gauge appears. While it is up, Death Pulse* damages the group, and if the gauge is not broken in time, Wave of Death* kills everyone. On Conquest it comes at around three-quarters health, and the groups in our pulls that survived broke the gauge at the very last moment. Every player should save stagger and control skills for this phase and use them together.

    Call: Break the gauge with stagger skills!, with a countdown on screen

  • ExplorationConquestSwarm Roar*

    During this long cast, Swarm Mark* repeatedly marks every player and Swarm Sting* water balls land where the players were standing. Keep moving for the entire cast and avoid stopping in the same place twice. On Conquest it is cast once, after the first wipe check.

    Call: Dodge!

  • ConquestLeap and two waves

    Bakarma casts on himself, and shortly after two waves roll out, one right after the other. Players should jump over both.

    Call: Avoid two Waves, Jump!

  • ConquestWinding Waterspout*

    At around a third of his health, waves form around Bakarma and keep coming. Jump over each wave as it reaches you.

    Call: Avoid Waves, Jump!

  • ConquestWave of Death: Call*, the second wipe check

    Right after Winding Waterspout*, Bakarma calls Wave of Death: Call* again, with the same gauge to break. This does not happen on Exploration. In our Conquest pulls, half of the groups that reached this point wiped here, so make sure stagger skills are off cooldown by the time Bakarma is at a third of his health.

    Call: Break the gauge with stagger skills!

If your runs show something different, please let us know on Discord.

About the ability names. The game does not show English names for most boss abilities, so we name them ourselves, either after what the ability does or as a translation from the Korean client. Names marked with a * are ours and not official. Abilities we have not named yet are described instead.
