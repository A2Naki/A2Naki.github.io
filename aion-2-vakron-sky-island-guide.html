AION 2 boss guide

Vakron Sky Island boss mechanics

Three bosses, each with an add to kill or a colour to read. Get those right and Vakron is the only real test.

Exploration is the easy version, Conquest the hard one. Calls are what the A2Naki boss mod says out loud; Kael stands for the player the boss picked.

Tiere

First boss

  • ExplorationConquestThe flower

    Tiere makes a flower grow. It dies to a few hits.

    Do: kill the flower first. Call: Kill Flower!

  • ExplorationGround zones

    Zones appear on the ground and keep pulsing. In our Exploration runs they did more damage to the group than anything else in the fight.

    Do: move out and stay out until they fade. Call: Move!

Thamon

Second boss

  • ExplorationConquestThe stone

    Thamon petrifies, and a stone appears. It breaks in a few hits.

    Do: break the stone. Call: Break the Stone!

  • ConquestRed glow

    Thamon glows red. While he does, melee hits heal him — a single melee player can give him back a big chunk of HP — while caster hits still hurt.

    Do: melee stops until the glow is gone; ranged and casters keep going. Call: Red, melee stop!

  • ExplorationConquestPurple glow

    The other way round: caster hits heal him and melee hits still hurt.

    Do: casters stop until the glow is gone; melee keeps going. Call: Purple, casters stop!

  • ExplorationThe circle and the group hit

    On Exploration the petrification also puts a circle on the ground, and another cast hits several players at once.

    Do: step out of the circle, spread for the hit. Call: Move!

Vakron

Last boss

  • ExplorationConquestThe red rock

    Vakron pulls the whole group to him, and then a very heavy hit lands.

    Do: get out right after the pull. Call: Get out away from boss!

  • ExplorationConquestSurging Thorns* — the red floor

    Waves run across a red floor.

    Do: jump over the waves. Call: Jump!

  • ConquestThe seed

    A cast at one player; then spikes root that player in place. Exploration doesn’t have it.

    Do: DPS down the seed to free the marked player. Call: Kael marked! DPS down the seed

  • ExplorationunconfirmedSmall zones

    Small zones on the ground.

    Do: step out. Call: Move!

Seen something different in your pull? Tell us on Discord — the guide and the boss mod are updated from your recordings.

About the spell names. The game doesn’t show English names for most boss spells, so we name them ourselves — after what the spell does, or translated from the Korean client. Every name with a * is one of ours, not the game’s. Spells we haven’t named yet are described by what they do.
