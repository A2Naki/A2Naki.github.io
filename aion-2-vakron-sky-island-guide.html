Vakron Sky Island boss mechanics

Vakron Sky Island has three bosses. Tiere and Thamon each have an add or a colour mechanic that the group has to handle, and Vakron himself is the main test.

Exploration is the easy difficulty and Conquest the hard one. The Calls under each mechanic are the warnings the A2Naki boss mod says out loud. Kael stands for the player the boss picks. Mechanics tagged unconfirmed have not yet been seen in one of our recordings on that difficulty.

Tiere

First boss

  • ExplorationConquestFlower

    Tiere summons a flower that dies to a few hits. Kill it before returning to the boss.

    Call: Kill Flower!

  • ExplorationConquestGround zones

    Zones appear on the ground and keep pulsing for a while. In our runs they dealt more damage to the group than anything else in the fight, so the group should stay together and move out of them as one, staying out until they fade.

    Call: Group up and move together!

Thamon

Second boss

  • ExplorationConquestStone

    Thamon petrifies and a stone appears, which breaks after a few hits. The group should break it.

    Call: Break the Stone!

  • ConquestRed glow

    While Thamon glows red, melee hits heal him, and a single melee player can restore a large part of his health. Caster hits still damage him. Melee players should stop attacking until the glow ends, while ranged players and casters continue.

    Call: Red, melee stop!

  • ExplorationConquestPurple glow

    The reverse of the red glow: caster hits heal Thamon and melee hits still damage him. Casters should stop attacking until the glow ends.

    Call: Purple, casters stop!

  • ExplorationCircle and group hit

    On Exploration the petrification also places a circle on the ground, and another cast hits several players at once. Step out of the circle and spread out for the hit.

    Call: Move!

Vakron

Last boss

  • ExplorationConquestRed rock

    Vakron pulls the entire group towards himself, and a very heavy hit follows. Move away from him immediately after the pull.

    Call: Get out away from boss!

  • ExplorationConquestSurging Thorns*, the red floor

    Waves travel across a red floor. Jump over them.

    Call: Jump!

  • ConquestSeed

    Vakron casts at a single player, and spikes then root that player in place. The rest of the group should focus the seed to free them. This mechanic does not appear on Exploration.

    Call: Kael marked! DPS down the seed

  • ExplorationunconfirmedSmall zones

    Small zones appear on the ground. Step out of them.

    Call: Move!

If your runs show something different, please let us know on Discord. The guide and the boss mod are updated from your recordings.

About the ability names. The game does not show English names for most boss abilities, so we name them ourselves, either after what the ability does or as a translation from the Korean client. Names marked with a * are ours and not official. Abilities we have not named yet are described instead.
