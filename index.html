A2Naki CompanionA live DPS meter and dashboard for AION 2

On any browser, a Corsair Xeneon Edge or right on top of the game: your fight, your party and your progress at a glance — so your eyes stay on the game.

Windows 10 / 11 · AION 2 Global

What it shows: DPS meter, boss warnings, training, party & timers

One screen per situation — in a fight, between fights, and on the long road of dailies. Every number comes from the game itself; if something isn’t known, it says so instead of guessing.

I · In the fight

Read the boss before it reads you

  • DPS & healing meterDamage, rDPS, healing, taken, skills and utility for the whole group, your own row in gold. Rates with two decimals, so 9.33k and 9.41k are not the same number.
  • Boss HP and cast barsHP with a trailing damage chunk, the stagger gauge, every cast as it happens — interruptible ones marked — and a kill projection that tells you whether you beat the enrage.
  • Spoken boss-mechanic warnings, boss by boss“Stop DPS soon!”, “Kael marked, move to Kael!”, “Kill the flag!”, “Avoid!” — written for each boss from real pulls, on screen and out loud, ahead of time where the boss gives it away (a cast, a debuff, a boss HP). You choose which and when.
  • Fight summary & historyKill or wipe, time vs your best, top damage and healing, deaths with the last hits that caused them — and who resurrected whom.
  • Dungeon & EventsSwitch from one pull to the whole dungeon run, world boss or field event, through respawns and group changes. Cleared runs keep your records — best DPS, best HPS and fastest clear — and your row shows how you’re doing against them.
  • Meter overlay in the gameJust the meter, in a small see-through window on top of the game. Clicks go straight through to the game; Ctrl+Shift+1–8 switch tabs. Needs Borderless or Windowed mode.
  • Copy to chatOne tap copies the list you’re looking at — damage, healing, taken or skills — as plain text, ready to paste into party chat or Discord.
II · Your group

Who needs you, and what’s about to run out

  • PartyHP and mana for everyone, low-HP and death alerts that stay until you tap them, people asking to join.
  • Buff timersBuffs, debuffs and cooldowns, shortest first; the last five seconds blink.
  • Combat StatsYour character’s stat block and deity points — only what the game actually sends.
III · Between fights

Progress that keeps itself

  • Kinah per hourEarned, spent and net for the session, with a sparkline.
  • CraftingLevels, recipes and materials as you work.
  • HomeworkDaily and weekly tasks per character, Od energy and tickets — cleared by itself at the reset.
  • World timersShugo Festival, invasions, rifts and sieges in server time and your local time, for your server region.
  • Reminders, out loud“Od energy is full”, the daily reset, Shugo, rifts, Arena — read aloud if you want, never in the middle of a fight. Off until you switch them on.
IV · Getting better

Train, compare, see what changed

  • Training on the dummyHit a Punching Bag or a Training Scarecrow and the target card becomes a training card: pick 30 s to 5 minutes, the clock starts on your first hit, and you see your DPS against your last run and your best — at the same second.
  • Every run kept with your setupRotation, combat power, item level, stats, buffs and gear are saved with each run. Pause and hit again for the next one; the last 100 runs per character stay.
  • Which piece changedPut two runs side by side: the rows that differ are marked, down to the gear slot you swapped or upgraded.
  • Analysis pageIn the browser: DPS over the fight with its axes, your rotation per skill with the gaps where a skill was ready and unused, two fights skill by skill, and what stopped you and for how long.

How it works

A small app in your Windows tray reads the data the game server already sends to your PC, and draws it. That’s the whole trick.

  1. Game serverSends your client the fight, party and world data, as always.
  2. Your PCNpcap lets the app receive a copy. Nothing is sent, injected or read from memory.
  3. 127.0.0.1The app talks only to your own dashboard, locked with a token only your Windows user can read.
  4. Browser, Edge or overlayOn any browser, as Widgets in iCUE, or as a meter on top of the game

Passive

Receive-only. It never sends anything to the game and never touches game files or memory.

Read-only

It shows what the game tells your PC — no automation, no macros; it never plays for you.

Stays on your PC

No account, no telemetry. Nothing leaves your PC except what you choose to send — like a bug report you upload yourself.

Still: NCSOFT may see reading game traffic as against the AION 2 terms of service, which is a risk to your account. Read Is it safe? before you decide.

Screenshots

No Edge? Any screen will do.

The browser page puts pages 1 and 2 together and fits any window — a second monitor, a laptop, or a tablet propped next to the keyboard.

It’s modular: you decide which windows you have open. Encounter, Party, Homework, World, Kinah and the others are separate widgets — keep only the ones you want, where you want them, from one meter on top of the game to the whole dashboard.
