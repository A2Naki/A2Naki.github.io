How AION 2 bosses work

The eight kinds of boss mechanics

Almost every mechanic in an AION 2 dungeon is one of these. Learn the kind and a new boss is half learned.

Zones and orbs

The boss puts something on the floor — orbs, circles, waves, fire — that pulses for a while. Step out and wait until it is gone.

Take cover

A room-wide hit with a safe spot: a stone, a pillar, the edge of a circle. The boss sets it up first, so there is time to get there.

Wipe checks

A gauge appears on the boss. Break it with stagger skills before the time runs out, or the whole group dies. Save your stagger skills for it.

Stagger and interrupts

The stagger (groggy) gauge drains with control skills; a broken gauge stops the cast. Interruptible casts are the ones to stop on purpose.

Reflect and counter stance

For a while, hitting the boss hurts you or heals him. Stop attacking — or only the wrong half of the group stops, as on Thamon.

Adds

A flower, a flag, a stone, a handmaiden. They usually die in a few hits and hurt a lot if left alone. Kill them first.

Marks and targets

One player is picked. Either they run out of the group, or the group stacks on them to share the hit. The mark tells you which.

Enrage timer

Dungeon bosses enrage if the fight runs too long. Watch the kill projection: if the kill comes after the enrage, burst now.

Questions

Boss mechanics, briefly

What are the boss mechanics in AION 2?

Dungeon bosses mix the eight kinds above: zones and orbs to step out of, group hits you hide from, wipe checks where the party must break the boss’s stagger gauge in time, reflect or counter stances, adds, marked or targeted players, and an enrage timer.

Is there a boss mod for AION 2?

Yes — A2Naki Companion. It reads the boss casts the game already sends to your PC and says short warnings out loud, ahead of time where it can. It never sends anything to the game. See the boss mod page, and read Is it safe? before you decide.

Where do these mechanics come from?

From our own recordings of real pulls on the Global servers: which cast starts a mechanic, what it does and who it hits. Where something is not confirmed yet, the page says so. The game doesn’t show English names for most boss spells, so we name them ourselves — after what they do, or translated from the Korean client; every name with a * is one of ours.
