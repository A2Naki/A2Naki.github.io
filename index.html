What it shows

Everything the moment needs.
Nothing it doesn’t.

One screen per situation — in a fight, between fights, and on the long road of dailies. Every number comes from the game itself; if something isn’t known, it says so instead of guessing.

I · In the fight

Read the boss before it reads you

  • Damage & healing meterDamage, healing, taken, skills and interrupts for the whole group, your own row in gold.
  • Boss HP and cast barsHP with a trailing damage chunk, the groggy gauge, every cast as it happens — interruptible ones marked — and a kill projection that tells you whether you beat the enrage.
  • Spoken boss-mechanic warnings“Take cover!”, “Avoid!”, “Move away from the orbs!” — on screen and out loud, a few seconds ahead. You choose which and when.
  • Fight summary & historyKill or wipe, time vs your best, top damage and healing, deaths — and who resurrected whom.
  • Dungeon & EventsSwitch from one pull to the whole dungeon run, world boss or field event, through respawns and group changes.
II · Your group

Who needs you, and what’s about to run out

  • PartyHP and mana for everyone, low-HP and death alerts that stay until you tap them, people asking to join.
  • Buff timersBuffs, debuffs and cooldowns, shortest first; the last five seconds blink.
  • Combat StatsYour character’s stat block and deity points — only what the game actually sends.
III · Between fights

Progress that keeps itself

  • Kinah per hourEarned, spent and net for the session, with a sparkline.
  • CraftingLevels, recipes and materials as you work.
  • HomeworkDaily and weekly tasks per character, Od energy and tickets — cleared by itself at the reset.
  • World timersShugo Festival, invasions, rifts and sieges in server time and your local time.

How it works

It listens. It never talks back.

A small app in your Windows tray reads the data the game server already sends to your PC, and draws it. That’s the whole trick.

  1. Game serverSends your client the fight, party and world data, as always.
  2. Your PCNpcap lets the app receive a copy. Nothing is sent, injected or read from memory.
  3. 127.0.0.1The app talks only to your own dashboard, locked with a token only your Windows user can read.
  4. Edge or browserWidgets in iCUE, or one page in Chrome / Edge on any screen.

Passive

Receive-only. It never sends anything to the game and never touches game files or memory.

Read-only

It shows what the game tells your PC — no automation, no macros; it never plays for you.

Stays on your PC

No account, no telemetry. Nothing leaves your PC except what you choose to send — like a bug report you upload yourself.

Still: NCSOFT may see reading game traffic as against the AION 2 terms of service, which is a risk to your account. Read Is it safe? before you decide.
