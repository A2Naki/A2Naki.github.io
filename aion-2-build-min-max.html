AION 2 build guide

Min-max your AION 2 build

Build guides tell you what should work best. We use the training dummy to find out what actually works best with our own gear and class.

Test a build in five steps

  1. Pick a run length. Hit a Punching Bag or a Training Scarecrow and the target card becomes a training card: choose 30 seconds to 5 minutes. The clock starts on your first hit and the run ends when the timer runs out; press Restart training for the next run.
  2. Set a baseline. Do three to five runs with what you have now. The card shows the average of your last five and how much they spread.
  3. Change one thing. One gear piece, one stigma, one skill upgrade, one step of the rotation. After a gear swap, change zone once, because that is when the game sends your new stats.
  4. Run again with the same length, buffs and number of runs.
  5. Compare. On the Analysis page, put two runs side by side. The rows that differ are marked, down to the gear slot you swapped. Give runs a label (“new gloves”) and compare the labels’ averages.

What to read

What to look at

Opener vs the rest

Your first 10 seconds against everything after. A strong opener with a weak tail is a cooldown problem, not a gear problem.

Idle time and unused skills

The card tells you while you hit: “2 s without a skill”, “Skill ready for 4 s”. The rotation chart shows every gap where a skill was ready and not used.

Crit and back attacks

Crit rate and back-attack rate per run, so a stat change and a positioning habit can be told apart.

Buff uptime

How much of the run each of your buffs was up. A buff that drops off mid-run costs more than most gear upgrades.

Gear, slot by slot

Combat power, item level, stats and every gear slot are saved with each run, up to the last 100 per character.

Your run vs your best

While you hit, your DPS is drawn against your last run and your best at the same second, so you know mid-run if it is going well.

Min-max rules

How we test

  • One change at a time. Two changes at once and you can’t tell which one helped.
  • Same length every time. A 30-second run rewards your opener; a 3-minute run shows your sustain. Pick the one that matches the content you play.
  • Beat the spread, not just the number. If the new average is up 2 % but your runs spread ±4 %, that is noise. Do more runs.
  • Same buffs. A party buff or food on one run and not the other ruins the test. The buffs of each run are saved, so check them.
  • Then test it on a boss. The dummy doesn’t move or make you dodge. Boss mechanics are where a rotation breaks; the DPS meter shows if it holds in a real pull.

Questions

Build FAQ

What is the best build in AION 2?

It depends on your class, your gear and the content. A build guide is a good starting point; the dummy tells you whether a change actually raised your DPS. Test it, keep what works and test the next change.

Which dummies count as training?

Punching Bags and Training Scarecrows, the melee one too. See How do I practise my rotation? in the FAQ.

Why are my stats the same after a gear swap?

The game sends your stats when you log in or change zone, not when you swap gear. Change zone once after swapping, then run.
