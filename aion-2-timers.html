    The schedule

    AION 2 event times on the Global servers

    The game runs on three clocks: resets on UTC+9 without daylight saving, rifts on Taiwan time (UTC+8), and the rest on your server region’s own clock, with daylight saving.

    EventWhenClock
    Daily reset16:00 every day (= 07:00 UTC)UTC+9
    Weekly resetWednesday 16:00 (= Wednesday 07:00 UTC)UTC+9
    Shugo FestivalEvery hour on the hour, over within about 30 minutesServer time
    Dimensional InvasionEvery hour at :30Server time
    Spacetime RiftEvery 3 hours: 02, 05, 08, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23 (level 45)UTC+8
    Arena of Tactics11:00–14:00 and 19:00–21:00Server time
    Artifact ConquestWednesday and Saturday 21:30 (still to be confirmed in game)Server time
    Od energy+15 every 3 hours: 01, 04, 07 … 22UTC+9

    Server regions: Europe (Central European time), NA East (US Eastern), NA West (US Pacific), South America (Brasília) and Japan. Spotted a wrong time? Tell us on Discord.

    Timers that speak — and remember

    • Reminders out loud: “Od energy is full”, the daily reset, Shugo, rifts, Arena — never in the middle of a fight, off until you switch them on.
    • Homework: daily and weekly tasks per character, Od energy and tickets, cleared by itself at the reset.
    • Field bosses live from the game, not from a guessed schedule.
