AION 2 Boss Mod

Boss mechanics, called out loud and on time — like the boss mods you know from other MMOs, written for AION 2 from real pulls.

What you hear

When the boss starts a mechanic, a voice says what to do — Take cover!, Dodge!, Kill Flower!, Kael marked, move to Kael! — and the same line shows under the boss HP with a countdown to when it lands. Wipe checks count down the time left: Break the gauge with stagger skills!

Where the boss gives it away early — a cast, a debuff on a player, an HP mark — the warning comes early too: Stop DPS soon!, Interrupt Berk Slam soon.

And when the kill projection shows the boss will outlive its enrage timer: Enrage before kill – burst now!

How it knows

Written per boss, learned on the rest

Written from real pulls

For the bosses in our dungeon guides each call is tied to the cast that starts the mechanic, with the seconds we measured in recorded fights — Exploration and Conquest separately.

Learned in the fight

On a boss it has never seen, it still finds group hits, adds, debuffs a healer must cleanse and casts worth interrupting — and remembers them for the next pull.

Stagger and interrupts

The stagger gauge is on the boss card all fight, and the interruptible cast that comes next is called ahead: Interrupt … soon.

Count boss casts

For learning a new boss: every spell gets a number the first time — 1, 2, 3 — and the voice says it each time it comes. Tell us “3 should say Get out” and the next version does.

Your choice

Set it up the way you raid

  • Voice on or off, volume and speed — the countdown on the training dummy follows the same volume.
  • How many seconds ahead the early warnings come.
  • One switch per kind: interrupt, stagger, wipe check, group damage, targeted, adds, cleanse, take cover, fire walls, stop attacking, enrage and orbs.
  • Where it shows: the boss card on a Xeneon Edge, in any browser, or at the top of the meter overlay in the game.

The dashboard speaks ten languages; the spoken boss warnings are in English.

Passive, read-only. The boss mod only receives a copy of what the game server already sends to your PC. It never sends anything to the game, never reads its memory and never plays for you. Questions

Boss mod FAQ

Is there a boss mod like DBM for AION 2?

A2Naki Companion works much like Deadly Boss Mods does in other MMOs: it watches the boss’s casts and calls the mechanic out loud with a countdown. It is free and runs next to the game on Windows 10 and 11.

Does it change the game?

No. It only receives a copy of the data the game server already sends to your PC — no injection, no memory reading, no automation. The risk that NCSOFT sees it as against the terms of service is still real; see Is it safe?

Which bosses does it know?

The bosses of Krao Cave, Draupnir Cave, Fire Temple, Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island have written calls. On the others it learns as you fight, and every recorded pull you share makes the next version better.

Does it come with a DPS meter?

Yes — the same app is a full AION 2 DPS meter for the whole party, with training on the dummy and world timers.
