AION 2 dungeon boss guide

Krao Cave boss mechanics

The first dungeon most groups run, and the first wipe check. Here is what Gadioton and Berk do on Exploration and on Conquest.

Exploration is the easy version, Conquest the hard one. Calls are what the A2Naki boss mod says out loud; a tag marked unconfirmed has not been seen in a recording on that difficulty yet.

About the spell names. The game doesn’t show English names for most boss spells, so we name them ourselves — after what the spell does, or translated from the Korean client. Every name with a * is one of ours, not the game’s. Spells we haven’t named yet are described by what they do.

Enhanced Harcon

First boss

Nothing in our pulls has needed a warning of its own. Interrupt what can be interrupted and keep his stagger gauge going down. The boss mod still picks up group hits, adds and cleansable debuffs as the fight runs.

Neglected Gadioton

Second boss

  • ExplorationExplosive Crystal*

    A blast that lands where you stand.

    Do: step out of it. Call: Dodge!

  • ExplorationCrao Energy*

    Four orbs appear around the room. They pulse, and then hit everyone still standing close to them.

    Do: stay clear of every orb until they are gone. Call: Move Away!

  • ConquestThe first cast at a player

    Gadioton picks one player and casts at them, more than once in a fight.

    Do: the targeted player moves out of it. Call: Avoid!

  • ConquestThe second cast at a player

    A different cast, also at one player, that keeps coming back.

    Do: the targeted player runs out of the group. Call: Run out!

Ultimate Berk

Last boss

  • ExplorationConquestunconfirmed on ConquestStones*, then Wave of Fury*

    Berk casts Stones* and three stones appear around the room. Then Wave of Fury* hits the whole room: whoever stands in the open takes a heavy hit or is stunned, whoever stands behind a stone takes nothing.

    Do: get behind a stone as soon as they appear and stay until the wave has hit. Calls: Take cover! at the stones, Find safe spot! when the wave starts (Exploration).

  • ExplorationThe hit near the boss

    A cast you don’t want to be next to Berk for — on Conquest the same spell picks a player (below).

    Do: move away from the boss. Call: Move away from boss!

  • ConquestPulses around him

    Berk casts on himself, then pulses damage all around him.

    Do: melee steps back until the pulses stop. Call: Run away from boss!

  • ConquestA player targeted

    Berk picks a player and hits them hard, again and again through the fight.

    Do: the named player runs out of the group. Call: Kael, run out! (with the player’s name)

  • ConquestThe wipe check: Extinction for Eternity*

    Near half HP Berk gets a fresh stagger gauge. If the group doesn’t break it in time, Extinction for Eternity* kills everyone.

    Do: save your stagger and control skills for this moment and break the gauge. Call: Break the gauge with stagger skills! with a countdown
