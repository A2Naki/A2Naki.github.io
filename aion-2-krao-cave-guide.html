AION 2 dungeon boss guide

Krao Cave boss mechanics

Usually the first dungeon people run, and on Conquest Berk has the first real wipe check. Two real bosses, Neglected Gadioton and Ultimate Berk, plus two minibosses on the way.

Exploration is the easy mode, Conquest the hard one. The Calls are what the A2Naki boss mod says out loud. Anything tagged unconfirmed we haven't caught in a recording on that difficulty yet.

Neglected Gadioton

First boss

  • ExplorationExplosive Crystal*

    Lands right where you're standing. Step off it.

    Call: Dodge!

  • ExplorationCrao Energy*

    Four orbs pop up around the room, pulse for a bit and then hit anyone still close. Stay away from all of them until they're gone.

    Call: Move!

  • ConquestThe first targeted cast

    He picks someone and casts at them, and it comes back more than once per pull. If it's you, sidestep.

    Call: Dodge!

  • ConquestThe second targeted cast

    Another one aimed at a single player. Take this one out of the group so nobody else gets hit.

    Call: Run out!

Ultimate Berk

Final boss

  • ExplorationConquestunconfirmed on ConquestStones*, then Wave of Fury*

    Berk casts Stones* and three stones show up around the room. Wave of Fury* follows and hits the whole room. In our pulls the player out in the open got a big hit or a stun, and the one behind a stone took nothing.

    Get behind a stone as soon as you see them and stay there until the wave is done.

    Calls: Take cover! when the stones appear, then Find safe spot! as the wave starts (Exploration).

  • ExplorationThe hit next to him

    You don't want to be standing next to Berk for this cast. On Conquest the same spell goes after one player instead (see below).

    Call: Move away from boss!

  • ConquestPulses around him

    He casts on himself and starts pulsing damage around him. Melee, back off until it stops.

    Call: Move away from boss!

  • ConquestOne player targeted

    Berk keeps picking a player and hitting them hard, over and over. Whoever he names runs out of the group.

    Call: Kael, run out! (with the player's name)

  • ConquestWipe check: Extinction for Eternity*

    Around half HP he gets a fresh stagger gauge. Break it in time or Extinction for Eternity* kills the whole group, so hold your stagger and control skills for it.

    Call: Break the gauge with stagger skills!, with a countdown on screen

Minibosses

Enhanced Harcon and Krao Seal Guardian

  • Enhanced Harcon

    Has his own stagger gauge. Nothing in our pulls needed a warning, so interrupt what you can and keep the gauge going down.

  • Krao Seal Guardian

    Stagger gauge as well. We haven't recorded anything worth a warning yet.

The boss mod still catches group hits, adds and debuffs to cleanse on these two while you fight. If one of them did something nasty in your run, post the recording in the beta on Discord.

Seeing something different in your runs? Tell us on Discord.

About the spell names. The game doesn't show English names for most boss spells, so we name them ourselves, either after what the spell does or translated from the Korean client. Every name with a * is ours, not the game's. Spells we haven't named yet are just described.
