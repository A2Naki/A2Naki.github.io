Krao Cave boss mechanics

Krao Cave is usually the first dungeon a group runs, and on Conquest Ultimate Berk introduces the first real wipe check. The dungeon has two bosses, Neglected Gadioton and Ultimate Berk, and two minibosses.

Exploration is the easy difficulty and Conquest the hard one. The Calls under each mechanic are the warnings the A2Naki boss mod says out loud. Mechanics tagged unconfirmed have not yet been seen in one of our recordings on that difficulty.

Neglected Gadioton

First boss

  • ExplorationExplosive Crystal*

    Explosive Crystal* is an explosion placed at a player’s position. Step out of it before it goes off.

    Call: Dodge!

  • ExplorationCrao Energy*

    Gadioton summons four orbs around the room. They pulse for a short time and then damage every player still standing near them, so the group should keep its distance from all four until they disappear.

    Call: Move!

  • ConquestFirst targeted cast

    Gadioton targets a single player with this cast several times per pull. The targeted player should step out of it.

    Call: Dodge!

  • ConquestSecond targeted cast

    A second cast aimed at a single player, which also returns throughout the fight. The targeted player should move out of the group so that nobody else is hit.

    Call: Run out!

Ultimate Berk

Final boss

  • ExplorationConquestunconfirmed on ConquestStones*, then Wave of Fury*

    Berk casts Stones*, which places three stones around the room, and follows it with Wave of Fury*, which hits the entire room. In our pulls, players standing in the open took heavy damage or were stunned, while players behind a stone took no damage.

    Move behind a stone as soon as they appear and stay there until the wave has passed.

    Calls: Take cover! when the stones appear, then Find safe spot! as the wave starts (Exploration).

  • ExplorationHit around the boss

    Berk uses this ability around himself, so players should not stand next to him when it is cast. On Conquest the same ability targets a single player instead (see below).

    Call: Move away from boss!

  • ConquestPulses around the boss

    Berk casts on himself and then pulses damage around him. Melee players should step back until the pulses stop.

    Call: Move away from boss!

  • ConquestSingle target

    Berk repeatedly targets one player with a heavy hit throughout the fight. The named player should run out of the group.

    Call: Kael, run out! (with the player’s name)

  • ConquestWipe check: Extinction for Eternity*

    At around half health Berk gains a new stagger gauge. If the group does not break it in time, Extinction for Eternity* kills every player, so it is important to keep stagger and control skills available for this phase.

    Call: Break the gauge with stagger skills!, with a countdown on screen

Minibosses

Enhanced Harcon and Krao Seal Guardian

  • Enhanced Harcon

    Enhanced Harcon has his own stagger gauge. None of his abilities in our pulls required a warning, so the focus is on interrupting what can be interrupted and keeping pressure on the gauge.

  • Krao Seal Guardian

    Krao Seal Guardian also has a stagger gauge. We have not recorded any ability that needs a warning yet.

The boss mod still detects group damage, adds and debuffs that need cleansing on both minibosses during the fight. If either of them did something dangerous in your run, please share the recording in the beta on Discord.

If your runs show something different, please let us know on Discord.

About the ability names. The game does not show English names for most boss abilities, so we name them ourselves, either after what the ability does or as a translation from the Korean client. Names marked with a * are ours and not official. Abilities we have not named yet are described instead.
