Changelog

What we changed in each version, newest first. The same list comes with every download as CHANGELOG.txt.

Version 0.2.36

2026-10-12

World: a new start page

  • The World window is now a portal. At the top it shows the time to the daily and to the weekly reset. Below that: the three nearest timers (events and field bosses about to spawn) and six tiles, **Timers, Dungeons, Homework, World bosses, Supply Request and Characters**. A tile opens its page and **‹ World** goes back. Nothing changes page by itself any more.
  • A tile glows while something there is still left to do.
  • Every number is what is **left** of its cap, the way the game counts its entries: Ascension Trial 0/3 means no tries left, Duty 2/5 means two missions still to do, a supply board 8/10 means eight cards still to deliver. Whatever has nothing left is **green** and says **Done**.
  • **Homework**: the Duty missions left today (as the game counts them), in progress, refreshes left, and the counters the game keeps: Daily Dungeon, Nightmare, Ascension Trial and Shugo Festival keys.
  • **Incursion of the Spirits** on the Homework page: your keys (they come back one a day, up to 7), whether you have done a run today, and when the next one starts (every hour at :30). The keys count as a task until today's run is done.
  • **Dungeons**: your Od, the entries left, and Remaining Unobtained Cube. At 0 Conquest cannot be entered until Wednesday, and the page says so.
  • **Substance Morph** on the Dungeons page: the Odyle Energy morphs left this week, per character (of 4) and per server (of 16). A recipe with morphs left counts as a task left.
  • **Expedition** on the Dungeons page: how many Exploration, Conquest and Transcendence runs you have done this week, and how many cubes are left to each dungeon's guaranteed reward (Conquest fills at 14, then it says "ready to collect" and the tile glows).
  • **Supply Request**: the four Abyss Supply Request boards, Emergency (10 a day), Weekly (10 a week), Regular: Craft and Regular: Boss (54 each), with how many cards are left to deliver and when a new board comes. A delivery that completes a card counts at once.
  • **World bosses**: the field bosses of your map, by name from the start. The game sends their times only while its **Map → Field Boss** list is open: open it once and the page shows the ones up (and for how long), then the next to spawn, with server and local time. The app keeps the times between starts (for your server only); times from a while ago say when they were seen, and a boss whose time has passed says **Due ≈**.
  • **Characters**: each character the app has seen on this PC, with what is left to do. Each row names exactly what its "N left" counts (Duty, Daily Dungeon, Nightmare, the keys …). Log in once on a character for the app to learn it. The app remembers them between starts. A value from before a reset is shown as it should be now and marked ≈ (checked against a real Wednesday reset: Shugo keys come back 3 a day, Nightmare 2 a day up to 14).

Encounter

  • When you enter a dungeon the meter shows the whole dungeon by itself and stays on it through every pull. Leaving the dungeon goes back to Fight; tap the chip to see the current pull.

Help

  • Help is new: the parts and their topics on the left, the chosen topic on the right, short and in all ten languages. It opens on the topic you read last.
  • An **i** beside some headings (the World pages, Farming's Balance, Combat Stats) explains them in a small box. **More in Help ›** opens the full topic, and **‹ Return** takes you back to where you were.

Getting started

  • The installer has a new choice, **Overlay over the game**, ticked from the start. It adds the shortcut **A2Naki Companion (overlay)** (Start menu, and the desktop with Desktop icon) and turns the overlay on when you click Finish. If A2Naki Companion is already running, the shortcut turns its overlay on.
  • When the game is live and nothing shows it yet, the tray says so once: **right-click the icon → Overlay**, or open **A2Naki Companion (browser)** on your desktop. A click on that notice opens the overlay.
  • The tray icon's text says **"right-click → Overlay"** while nothing is open, and counts the open windows ("3 open") instead of "widgets".

Overlay

  • The overlay settings say in one line what each window shows (also when you point at a window's button).
  • On a locked window the meter's own Copy button is no longer shown: a locked window lets every click through to the game, so it could never be tapped there. Copy with the small Copy button beside the padlock, or unlock the window.

Boss warnings

  • Krao Cave, Neglected Gadioton (Exploration and Conquest): "Move away from boss!" 2.3 s before his blast around himself. The call comes on the cast that always precedes it, so there is time to step out.
  • Krao Cave Conquest, Enhanced Harcon: "Dodge!" when he summons the ground explosions (once per pair of casts).
  • New: **"Get out!"** when you stand in a boss's ground effect (a pool or field that keeps hitting you). Only you hear it, after about a second in it, and again every 6 seconds while you stay. Walking through says nothing. It follows the Orbs switch in Warnings.

Farming

  • In the window choices (the overlay's settings and the browser page) Farming is now called **Farming Pets**, so the pets are easy to find.
  • The Pets page now says what its numbers are: **Balance** on top (every soul your pets have collected, then your soul crystals per genus), then **This session** with Souls gained, Levels gained and Crystals gained.
  • Crystals gained counts only the crystals your pets bring in. A reward of crystals (1,000 at once) made it jump; it still adds to your balance.

Kinah & Crafting

  • The page dots stay in the same place on every page: on a crafting page they no longer jump down from where they sit on the Kinah page.
  • Your professions keep the order you first crafted them in. Crafting in another profession no longer swaps the pages, so you stay on the one you were looking at.

Licence

  • The licence now says outright that the data files (boss rules, timings, schedules) are part of the app: they may not be copied out or republished.

Version 0.2.34

2026-10-12

Encounter

  • The buffs and debuffs on the target card are right now: your group's debuffs no longer show as the boss's own "Buff". Each one has its name (from the skill that put it on) and an icon for what it does: damage over time, slow, heal block, mark, delayed damage, control. The boss's own come first, then yours, then your group's, with the seconds left ("8 s") and a thin bar that drains. Effects the game itself hides, Groggy (the stagger bar shows it) and anything without a name are left out.
  • With the keyboard (Enter / Space), the focus now stays where you are: a skill row opens and closes again, the boss guide and a death's last hits toggle, a fight opened from History puts the focus on "‹ History", and going back puts it on that fight. Before, the focus jumped to the start of the page.

Stability

  • A window that hits an error once no longer stays closed for the evening: it skips that moment and goes on. Only an error that keeps coming (three times within 10 seconds) shows "This window hit an error", and the other windows always keep working.

Overlay

  • The overlay settings say what this window shows, as a row of choices: pick another and the same window changes in its place, with its size, opacity and background. "+ a window" adds a second window, and "Close this window" closes one of two. Before, ticking a box opened a new window when you meant to change this one.
  • The overlay windows snap to each other and to the screen's edge while you move or resize them (unlocked): side by side with a small gap, or lined up at the top, bottom or side.
  • Unlocking a window no longer changes its size: the frame and the Lock bar lie on top of it, so two windows stay lined up when you lock them again.

Kinah & Crafting

  • The page dots no longer cover the session's number: they sit at the end of the "Session" line, also with many crafting pages.

Version 0.2.33

2026-10-11

Thanks

  • Baka, for recordings of Shattered Arkanis (level 3) that the boss calls are built from.
  • Olaaris, for a recording of Fire Temple (Conquest) with all three bosses.

Analysis

  • The numbers on the left of the DPS chart and the training chart are no longer cut off: 14.80k could read as 4.80k.
  • The fights are one list: tap a row to show that fight, and Compare on another row to compare the two. Training runs work the same way, and their side-by-side view shows once you pick a run to compare with.
  • What to improve is graded high / medium / low, and its diamonds are no longer cut at the edge.
  • The rotation's "ready, not used" has a small sample of the shaded block instead of the word "amber".

Encounter

  • PvP mode stays on when an enemy player kills you and you come back on the same map. It ends when you do something else: hit a monster, press Restart training, or go to another map.
  • A fight's length is counted to its last hit everywhere: the Summary, History and the session now show the same time and DPS as the meter. The game reports a death up to 4 seconds after the killing hit, which made short fights look longer and their DPS lower.
  • A fight on a training dummy is always summed up ("Ended") and kept in History, wherever you train. Before it depended on the monsters standing nearby.
  • A wipe ends the fight: when the whole group is dead, the pull is summed up as a wipe and the next pull is a new fight. Before, the wipe and the kill after it could be one long fight (your old character id from before a zone change still counted as alive).
  • Utility tab: a cast counts as "went through" only when its skill can be interrupted by the same rule the cast bar and the voice use (the boss data, or what the app learned about that boss). Skills of adds and casts cut by a stagger break are no longer counted as interruptible.
  • Utility tab: a debuff counts as a missed cleanse only when it can be cleansed by the same rule the voice uses: the boss data names it, or your group took it off at least twice and at least half of the times it came. A debuff someone took off once no longer makes every later one a "miss".
  • A training attempt you drop with Restart training is not kept anywhere.
  • A new fight switches the meter back to Fight, also when you were looking at the whole dungeon run.
  • A zone change starts the meter afresh in full: the last fight's casts no longer show on the training card after a zone change, and a skill used just before a teleport no longer counts as the opener of the first fight on the other side.
  • A fight opened from History shows in a meter list (never Utility), and the button by the tabs says "‹ History" and takes you back to the list.
  • The finished training card says what its numbers mean: "DPS: first 10 s … · rest …", and the skill line reads "+14.1 % vs last run".
  • The training card says "Buff uptime", and Restart training and the Analysis button are the same size and look; the Analysis button says "Analysis (new window)".
  • On a dungeon run, the line above the meter ("… best clear 11:30 · party …") is no longer cut off by the Copy button: it uses two lines when it needs them.

Boss calls

  • Vakron's red rock (Vakron Sky Island) now says "DPS down red stone!" (it said "Get out away from boss!").
  • Vakron (Vakron Sky Island, Conquest) says "Move!" for his two ground zones, as on Exploration.
  • Judge Urahum (Urugugu Canyon, Conquest) also says "Dodge!" for a second spell.
  • "Group damage!" is gone: the app no longer says it for skills that hit the whole group. Boss calls are chosen by hand.

Party

  • A player who joins your group shows at once: before, the group could read 3/5 for a few minutes while it was full.

Stability

  • One window that hits an error no longer stops the others: it shows "This window hit an error" in its own frame, and every other window keeps working.
  • Each widget now carries only the code it shows (the Character widget no longer loads the Encounter's), so the widgets are smaller and one window's problem cannot reach the others. Every packed widget is opened on its own and checked before a version ships.

Farming

  • Pets with the keyboard: opening a pet puts the focus on "‹ Pets", and going back puts it on that pet's row, so you can go on with Tab. Before, the focus jumped to the start of the page.

Version 0.2.32

2026-10-10

PvP

  • Encounter switches to PvP by itself when you fight players of the other faction: the enemies in the fight (class, HP, your damage on them and theirs on you, the one hurting you most), a warning when you lose a lot of HP fast and who did it, your best 5 seconds against players, your kills, assists and deaths, your Abyss Points with what you gained this session, and who killed you last with which skill. Enemies who fall are marked dead. If the app was started after you logged in, Abyss Points "this session" shows "–" until your next login rather than a number that is too small. It goes back to the target card when you fight monsters again. Names are only shown, never saved.

Overlay

  • Overlay settings moved onto the overlay: the tray menu only turns it on and off ("Overlay"). Click the padlock to unlock it, and the gear on its top bar opens the settings: opacity, background, the Copy button, reset position and size, and a list of the shortcuts.
  • The overlay can show one or two windows at once: tick them on its settings page — the DPS meter, the target card (HP, cast, buffs, the boss warning and kill time), Training, PvP, Farming, World, Kinah & Crafting and Status. Each window has its own place, opacity and background.
  • In the Farming overlay the pet that got the last soul is on top with what is left to its next level and this session's souls under it, so you see it without clicking while you farm.
  • The meter overlay shows only the meter again, also while you train on a dummy: Training is its own overlay window now.
  • The PvP overlay window shows the PvP card alone, and a waiting line until you fight players.
  • With two overlay windows, a crash in one only restarts that window; the other stays as it is.

Training

  • The chart on the training card now shows how far ahead of (green) or behind (orange) your best run you are, in percent, second by second. A gain of a few percent is easy to see, and the chart stays on the card when the run is done.
  • The training timer is the fight's length: when it runs out the fight ends, so Analysis, History and the meter count the same 30 seconds (or 1, 2, 3, 5 minutes) as the card. Hits after the timer are not counted until you stop for a moment or press Restart training.
  • The training card no longer switches to an HP bar when another player's area attack hits your old training dummy after you stopped.

Accessibility

  • The widgets work better with a keyboard and a screen reader: buttons have names in all ten languages, the page indicator is a button, small buttons are easier to hit, and marks such as the top-damage warning are also said in words.

Analysis

  • THIS FIGHT now shows your total damage in the fight (not per second) next to the whole group's.
  • Damage taken by enemy attack shows the attacks' names instead of their numbers, like the target card does.

Analysis

Boss calls

  • Tiere (Vakron Sky Island, Exploration and Conquest): the ground zones now say "Group up and move together!".
