Version 0.2.28

2026-10-09

Boss calls

  • Tiere (Vakron Sky Island, Exploration and Conquest): the ground zones now say "Group up and move together!".
  • One call when the boss picks a player to run out: "Kael, run out!", with the player's name. Divine Auldor's grab said "Kael targeted, move out!" before, and Neglected Gadioton (Krao Cave, Conquest) said only "Run out!". When we cannot know who it is, the call stays "Run out!".
  • "Stop DPS!" when hitting the boss hurts you or heals him, in every language. It said "Stop attacking" before.
  • When you are the one who must run out, the call is just "Run out!" (it said "You, run out!").
  • Heartless Mozant (quest miniboss): "Kill the flag!" when his flag appears, like Scouter Sahar and Personal Guard Irima, and "Kill the totem!" when his healing totem appears.
  • Ultimate Berk (Krao Cave, Exploration): when he casts the move away hit, we only say "Move away from boss!". No interrupt call, no INTERRUPT tag on the cast bar and no "Break the boss now" for it any more.
  • Ultimate Berk (Krao Cave, Exploration): only the stones speak, "Take cover!". The wave after them no longer says "Find safe spot!".

Pets

  • Homework is now called Farming. Its first page is the new Pets page, and today's Homework is the second page. The Pets page shows every pet you got souls for this session, the latest on top, with its level, the souls it has toward the next level and the souls you got today. Above it: the session's souls, levels and soul crystals. The app reads it all from the game by itself and remembers your pets between starts.

Encounter

  • Training: the card's timer is now the set everywhere. A set ends when the timer runs out, and the card and Analysis show the same DPS for it. A set the fight ended before the timer is marked "stopped" in Analysis. Without the timer, a set ends 6 seconds after your last hit on the dummy: healing or an aura on someone else no longer keeps it going. Sets you already saved are fixed too, and the Analysis chart now shows its scale.
  • Skills and Analysis show names instead of numbers for 81 more skills, among them every class's basic attacks and the Cleric's Discharge (it showed "Skill 17030000").
  • The Analysis button now sits in the same place in every view: at the bottom right of the boss side, on the boss card, the guide and the training card. "Restart training" stays where it was.

Privacy

  • Our research notes (which recordings and dates a boss call was learned from) are no longer built into the program.

Open Beta

  • The About box of the Open Beta reads "Open Beta · expires <date>".

Version 0.2.27

2026-10-08

One call per mechanic

  • "Dodge!" for anything that lands where you stand (no more "Avoid!").
  • "Move!" for zones and orbs on the floor.
  • "Move away from boss!" when you need distance from the boss (Ultimate Berk, Guardian Captain Raur).
  • "Get out away from boss!" for Vakron's red rock and Judge Urahum.
  • "Adds!" for all adds, whether they are being summoned or already up.
  • "Avoid two Waves, Jump!" for Bakarma's leap on Conquest.

Boss calls

  • Vakron (Conquest): the seed says "Kael marked! DPS down the seed", with the marked player's name.
  • Kromede's Desire: the fire walls say "Fire walls! Find the gap".

Training

  • The countdown on the training dummy is a 3, 2, 1 metronome tick with a higher tick on GO, instead of a voice. It follows your Warnings volume.
  • The "Press Restart training for a new run" hint is gone.

Krao Cave

  • Enhanced Harcon counts as a miniboss, like Krao Seal Guardian, so the dungeon progress shows two bosses.

Open Beta

  • Our first open beta: anyone can use it and share the invite to our Discord.

Version 0.2.26

2026-10-08

Training

  • A Restart training button. A new run only starts when you press it, and a finished run stays on the card until then.
  • A spoken countdown before each run. Stop hitting for 4 seconds and the run stops.

Settings and Analysis

  • The boss mechanic switches have their own Mechanics tab; Warnings keeps the voice settings.
  • The Analysis button is always at the bottom of the Encounter. On the Xeneon Edge it opens the Analysis page in your PC's browser.
  • A Notes field at the top of the Analysis page.

Boss calls

  • Krao Cave (Exploration): Explosive Crystal* on Neglected Gadioton, Wave of Fury* and the hit next to Ultimate Berk.
  • Krao Cave (Conquest): Gadioton's two targeted casts, and Berk's pulses and targeted hit.
  • Draupnir Cave (Conquest): Phantasmal Lakshmi's fast hit; Bakarma's leap is called earlier.
  • Urugugu Canyon (Conquest): Raur's targeted cast, Divine Auldor's Wing Strike* and Snatch*.

Version 0.2.25

2026-10-07

Training

  • "1.8 s without a skill" on the card and "Idle!" out loud when you stop pressing skills.
  • The skill that stood ready longest without being used.
  • A pace chart: this run against your best run of the same length.
  • A training voice that counts you in and says how the run went (its own switch under Warnings).
  • Your opener (first 10 seconds) against the rest of the run, crit and back-attack rates, buff uptime and the average of your last five runs.
  • Your own labels per run on the Analysis page, with an average and a best per label.
  • A run started before the app knew your name is kept and given to your character at the next zone change.

Boss calls

  • Count boss casts (off by default): every boss spell gets a number the first time it comes, and the voice says it each time. Useful when learning a new boss.
  • Judge Urahum says "Get out away from boss!" and nothing else.
  • Divine Auldor: Wing Strike* gets a call; Whirlwind Shot* is quiet.
