Privacy

Your data, plainly

The beta runs on our Discord, with no sign-up form. The app itself sends nothing anywhere, except the optional beta features below.

  • Sign-upNone any more: you join our Discord and download the beta there.
  • Earlier formAnswers from the old sign-up form are deleted when the beta ends, sooner if you ask.
  • Beta extrasOnly what you choose to send (bug reports, recordings, boss data), deleted after days.

Joining the beta

Since 7 October 2026 there is no sign-up form: the beta runs on our Discord server, where you download the builds. We collect nothing to let you in. What you post on Discord is covered by Discord’s privacy policy; we read it only to improve the app.

The earlier sign-up form

Until then, testers applied with a form. If you filled it in, this is what we have:

  • Your e-mail address — only to send the beta invite to the testers we chose.
  • Your Discord name — so we can recognise you when you join the beta channels on our Discord.
  • Your form answers — server / region, main class and level, what you mainly do in the game, hours per week and time zone, Xeneon Edge yes / no, Windows version, game language, whether you have used a meter before, and that you understand the risks.

We don’t ask for your real name, your character ID or anything else. Please don’t put more in the free-text fields than you’re happy to share.

Why

To choose testers who play differently from each other, and to contact the ones we picked. Nothing else: no newsletter, no marketing, and we never sell or share your details.

The form ran on Google Forms, so Google stores the answers for us under Google’s privacy policy. Only the project team can see them.

How long we keep it

  • Answers from the earlier form: deleted when the beta ends, or sooner if you ask us on Discord.
  • Recordings and bug reports that testers send during the beta are only used to improve the app and are deleted after analysis. You choose when to record.

Your rights — and how to ask

You can ask at any time to see what we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it — including withdrawing your application. Write to us on our Discord server and we’ll do it as soon as we can, at the latest within 30 days. If you think we got something wrong, you can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Contact us

Questions about your data, a request to see, correct or delete it, or anything else about this policy: write to us on our Discord server. Tell us the Discord name or e-mail you signed up with — or, for shared boss data, the install id the app showed you — so we can find your data. You can also send us a private message there.

The app itself

A2Naki Companion sends nothing (except the optional beta features below, only when you choose them, and the beta build check below). No account, no telemetry, no analytics. It talks only to your own dashboard on your own PC (127.0.0.1). Your settings, logs and any recordings stay on your PC, and a bug report is a file you share yourself — or don’t.

Beta: sharing learned boss data (optional)

In beta builds only, a tester can switch on Share learned boss data. It is off until they say yes. Then, once a day, the app sends our server what it learned about bosses — boss, skill, debuff and monster ids with counts (including how often a boss skill hit you hard), nothing else — with a random number for that install and the app version. We keep the install's newest upload with its date (no time of day), the app version and the ids and counts — no names and no character data, and we do not store or read your IP address; Cloudflare, which runs our server, sees it in transit under its own privacy policy. Your upload is deleted 30 days after the last one, so also 30 days after you switch sharing off (that stops new uploads at once). To have them deleted sooner, send us the install id the app shows when you switch it on, on our Discord server (see Contact us). We use the data only to improve the boss warnings in the next version.

Beta: sending a bug report (optional)

In beta builds only, after you create a bug report you can choose Send to A2Naki. The app shows what it will send and sends nothing until you confirm. It sends a copy of the report: the app’s logs from the last days, the app, Windows and .NET versions, your on/off settings, and — if you ticked it — the learned boss data. Before the file is made, your Windows user name and PC name are removed (a name of only one or two letters only inside folder paths, so ordinary words in the log stay readable), and every folder path is cut down to its last two parts, for example …\recordings\x.a2rec. With it go the app version and the same random install number as above. We keep the report with its date (no time of day) and give you a report number such as A2-7F3KQ2; we do not store or read your IP address (Cloudflare sees it in transit, as above). Only the developers read reports, only to find and fix the bug. Every report is deleted after 14 days. To have one deleted sooner, send us its report number on our Discord server (see Contact us).

The recording (only if you tick it). In the same step you can tick Include the recording (off by default). The app then also sends the newest part of your recording, packed. A recording holds the game traffic around you while it ran: your character and its stats, the names and actions of other players near you (group members and passers-by), and network addresses (your PC and the game server). It is stored with the same report number, read only by the developers to find the bug, never published, and deleted after 7 days — sooner if you ask on our Discord server. Please tell your group when you record a dungeon run.

Beta: the build check

Beta builds only (a normal release never does this): when the app starts, and then every 6 hours while it runs, it asks our server whether this beta build may still be used. This is a network contact even when you share nothing. It sends only the build name (the short form of the tester name the build was made for, as in the About box, e.g. liberta) and the app version — nothing else about you, your PC or your game. The server answers “ok” or “switched off” with a short message, and stores nothing about the request: no log, no IP address, no time (Cloudflare, which runs it, sees your IP address in transit under its own privacy policy, as above). If the server cannot be reached, the app keeps working; the build’s end date is still the hard stop. If a build is switched off, the app stops reading the game and shows the message in the tray and the About box; it remembers that on your PC (one small file in %APPDATA%\A2Naki) until the server says “ok” again.

This website

This site itself uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It embeds nothing from other sites (the beta page links to our Discord; nothing loads from there until you click). It remembers your Elyos / Asmodian choice in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits for security, see GitHub’s privacy statement.
