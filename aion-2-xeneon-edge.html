Corsair Xeneon Edge

AION 2 on the Xeneon EdgeFree iCUE widgets for AION 2

We made three page widgets that turn Corsair’s 2560 × 720 touchscreen into an AION 2 dashboard with the boss and its mechanics, the DPS meter, your party, timers and FPS.

Get the beta on Discord Set it up

Windows 10 / 11 · iCUE 5.47+ · AION 2 Global

Three pages

What is on each page

Each page fills the whole Edge. You only need to swipe when you change what you are doing, never in the middle of a pull.

  • 1 · Group

    The boss card with HP, stagger gauge, cast bar and spoken mechanic warnings; the DPS meter with damage, healing, taken, skills and history; your buffs and cooldowns; the party with HP and alerts.

  • 2 · Progress / World

    Farming with your pets’ souls and levels and the Homework for every character, Od energy and tickets, world timers for Shugo, Invasion and Rift, field bosses, Kinah per hour and crafting.

  • 3 · Performance

    FPS with a graph, CPU and GPU load and temperature, VRAM, ping and the companion’s own status, from iCUE’s sensors.

Setup

How to set it up

  1. Install A2Naki and Npcap. The setup is in the beta on our Discord; Npcap comes from npcap.com, and the default options are fine.
  2. Import the widgets. At the end of setup click Import all three widgets into iCUE… (or later from the tray menu). iCUE asks once per widget, so click Import each time.
  3. Make three pages on the Edge in iCUE and drag one A2Naki page widget onto each: Group, Progress / World, Performance.
  4. Set the size to XL under Appearance → Size, so each widget fills the screen.

Touch works the way you expect: tap a tab to switch it, tap an alert to clear it. Nothing needs a touch during combat.

No Edge? The same dashboard runs in any browser, on a second monitor, a laptop or a tablet, and the DPS meter can sit on top of the game as a see-through overlay. CPU, GPU and FPS need iCUE, so they only show on the Edge.

Questions

Xeneon Edge FAQ

Does AION 2 support the Xeneon Edge?

Not by itself. A2Naki Companion, a free fan project, adds the three iCUE page widgets for AION 2. It is not made or endorsed by NCSOFT or Corsair.

Which iCUE version do I need?

iCUE 5.47 or newer, on Windows 10 or 11. Importing only adds the widgets to iCUE’s list (group “A2Naki”); you place them yourself.

Is it safe for my account?

The app is passive: it receives a copy of what the game server sends to your PC and never sends anything back or touches the game. NCSOFT may still see reading game traffic as against the terms of service, so please read Is it safe? first.
