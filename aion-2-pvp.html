AION 2 PvP meter

When you fight players of the other faction, the A2Naki app turns its Encounter window into a PvP card by itself: who is in the fight, how hurt they are, who is hurting you, and how your session goes. Nothing to switch on, nothing to type in.

The PvP card

What it shows

  • The enemies in the fight: each player's class, an HP bar, the damage you dealt them (→) and the damage they dealt you (←). Someone who falls is marked dead.
  • Who hurts you the most: their row is marked, so you know whom to deal with first.
  • Burst on you: a red line when you lose a lot of HP in a few seconds, with how much, how fast and who did it.
  • Your best 5 seconds of damage on players.
  • Kills, assists and deaths, and who killed you last with which skill.
  • Abyss Points: your total and what you gained this session.

On and off

When the PvP card shows

It turns on when you or your group hit, or are hit by, a player of the other faction. It stays on without a timer, also when an enemy kills you and you come back on the same map, and goes back to the normal target card when you:

  • hit a monster again,
  • press Restart training, or
  • go to another map.

In the Abyss, where you fight players and monsters in the same seconds, a monster hit ends it only after a short while without any PvP hit, so the card does not flip back and forth.

Where

On the Edge, in a browser or over the game

The PvP card takes the place of the target card in the Encounter window, next to the DPS meter, in a browser or on a Xeneon Edge. You can also open it as its own overlay window over the game: it then shows the PvP card alone, with a waiting line until you fight players.

Abyss Points "this session" are counted from your login. If you started the app after you logged in, it shows "–" until your next login, rather than a number that is too small.

Privacy

Names are never saved

The names of the players you fight are only shown while you fight. They are never written to a file or sent anywhere, and the fight summary keeps no enemy names. A2Naki only reads the game's traffic on your own PC; it never sends anything to the game. See privacy.
